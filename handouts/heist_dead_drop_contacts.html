Six Friends Inside

A manifest of the agents you may call upon for the evening's work at the palace. They do not know each other. Keep it that way.

Leafcutter Ant · Major

Tudd

A soldier who outlived his unit and is still very angry about it.

Tough at the door, tougher at the wall. Knows which keystones crack under a charge and which rooms have only one way out. Bring him when you need a thing made loud, or a thing put up in a hurry.

UseDirected violence · demolition · field engineering.

“Point and I'll bring it down. Don't point twice.”

Leafcutter Ant · Minum

Demicous

A brood-nurse the guards have stopped seeing.

Goes anywhere a caretaker is permitted, which is anywhere with a child or a feeding-ladle within arm's reach. Will carry a parcel in. Will not stand beside you if anything comes loose.

UseAccess · couriering · quiet observation.

“I'm only — I'm only fetching. Excuse me, please.”

Jewel Beetle · Bartender

Vikko

Hears more in an hour than the guard captain hears in a week.

Behind the long bar all evening, listening twice for every pour. Will short a drink, dose a glass, or walk a small parcel through the kitchen door if the parcel is small and the favour is square.

UseIntel · poisoning a drink · smuggling supplies via service entrance.

“Tell me what you're drinking. I'll tell you what your trouble is.”

Lacewing · Performer

Diedre

Holds a room the way a hawk holds a thermal.

Takes the lower stage at the third bell. When she opens her wings, every face in the hall turns to her. The retinue she walks on and off is whoever she's told to bring — move in her wake and you move unseen.

UseDistraction · cover · moving bodies in or out with her troupe.

“I can hold a room for ninety counts. Don't waste them.”

Cockroach · Cutman

Dax

Has stitched every fighter on tonight's card at least once.

Runs the kit-room behind the Mixed Mandible Arts pit. Keeps a clean needle, two kinds of poultice, and the only map between the cage doors anyone trusts. Friends with the fighters; can put you in front of any of them.

UseMedical aid · introductions to any cage-fighter on the card.

“Everything bleeds. Most things stop. Sit down.”

Sawfly Larva · Mixologist

Zalle

Pours weather, not drinks.

Tends the pheromone lounge off the east hall. Can perfume the air within three paces of any guest — sleepy, brash, weepy, agreeable — for as long as a glass remains in their hand. Discreet. Expensive. Worth it.

UseMood manipulation · mild status effects on a chosen guest.

“Pick a feeling. I'll pour the room into it.”
