# Canopia — Return Scenes (DM Reference)

Three polished, DM-facing scene chunks for the party's return to Canopia after the
meadow/mantis clear: the reunion with Beeatrix at the Dewdrop, the eastern promenade
view of the Red Ant Queen's palace, and the bugsmith finishing Tabby's mantis blades.
Read-aloud text is in blockquotes; mechanics and intel are called out inline. Each chunk
ends with its own loose ends / decisions surfaced.

---

# DEWDROP INN — Return to Beeatrix

**When:** Late evening, party returning from the meadow (mantis cleared)
**Where:** Dewdrop Inn, Canopia
**Who's here:** Beeatrix (on stage), Dale (behind the bar), a thin scatter of patrons
**Purpose of scene:** Emotional payoff for the mantis hunt · reward handoff · seed the fete/heist

---

## 1 · Approach (read-aloud)

> You come up on the Dewdrop in the late evening. After the glitz of the city around it, the bar looks all the cozier for its humble glow — warm light leaking through the windows, dim and low. Faint through the door drifts a song: gentle chords, a melancholy tune you can't quite place.

## 2 · Through the Door (read-aloud)

> Inside, the lights are turned down and the crowd is thin. Dale leans against the bar, not working — just fixed on the little stage in the corner. The voice carrying the song is clear and sad and *beautiful*, and it takes you a moment to place it.
>
> It's Beeatrix. Up on the stage, crooning into the dark.

**Dale:** Solemn while he thinks no one's watching. The moment he clocks the party, his demeanor shifts to *quietly amused* — he knows what they did, and he knows what it's about to do to her.

---

## 3 · The Entrance — Stealth Check

Let the party choose to slip in or stride in. If they're sneaking:

| Roll (DC) | Result |
|-----------|--------|
| **Stealth 12+** | Beeatrix notices them mid-song |
| **Stealth 15+** | Only Dale notices |
| **Stealth 20+** | No one clocks them; they can watch the whole song unseen |

> **DM note:** The check only sets *who sees them and when*. The song lands either way — the question is whether Beeatrix breaks during it (she sees them) or gets to finish (she doesn't).

---

## 4 · The Song

She's singing about brave, kind bugs — the sort you meet once in a lifetime — and how she sent them off to their certain deaths while she was at the bottom of a bottle. It's a lament, and it's about the party.

**If the party interrupts the song:**
> She looks at them. Shock holds her face for a single beat — then it crumples, and she starts bawling.

**If the song is allowed to finish:**
> Beeatrix heaves a long, sad sigh, steps down off the stage, knocks back a shot of whiskey at the bar, and goes to her table to start packing her harp into its case — moving like someone who's already grieved you.

---

## 5 · The Reunion — Weeping → Recovering

Play it as a slide from guilt to disbelief to gratitude. Beats to hit, in order:

1. *"I didn't — I thought for sure — I'm so, so, so, so sorry…"*
2. *"Are you okay? What **happened**? How did you get away?"*
3. *"You **killed** it?? I — I didn't think that was possible. How can I ever repay you?"*

> **DM note — the truth of the monster:** It was an *orchid mantis family* (a mother and her nymphs), not a lone beast. If the party tells her that, give her a reaction — these are the predators of the Overgrowth made suddenly, domestically real. ([Open question — her response is unwritten.])

---

## 6 · The Reward

**Non-monetary token — offered freely:**
The last of her late mother's **Stick-All Honey.**
> *Sticks anything to anything. Forever.* (Heirloom; she's giving away the last of her mom — let that land.)

**Gold:** ⚠️ **[TBD — pick a number before the table reaches this beat.]** Prep never set the amount; it's still open after the meadow clear.

---

## 7 · The Fete / Heist Threads

Surface these naturally once the mood lifts:

- **The Red Ant Queen's parties:** She's heard of them. She did **not** know the fête was actually happening — she's been on a bit of a binge and lost the thread.
- **Performing at the fête:** *Very* willing to front the band if asked. (Note: Nancy already recruited her at breakfast back in Session 6 — this is the follow-through / confirmation.)

### 🎭 DM NOTE — Beeatrix as a Heist Asset (or Liability)

A live wire for the day of the heist:

- May show up **sober**… but nerves hit fast, and she'll start drinking almost immediately.
- Passes a flask around the crew **before** they go in, then starts **ordering drinks** and getting visibly drunk once inside.
- Left unmanaged, she **dramatically crashes out** — a scene.
- **Managing her** = Persuasion / Intimidation, **high DC.**
- 💡 A crash-out could be turned into a *deliberate distraction* if the party reads it that way. Asset or liability is on them.

---

## 8 · Wrap

The party can **crash here for the night**… but remind them: **Canopia stays open late.** If they've got more they want to do before the city sleeps, the door's right there.

---

### Loose ends / decisions surfaced
- **Beeatrix's gold reward** — set the number.
- **Her reaction to "it was a family of mantises"** — decide if/how she takes it.
- **Sober or drunk at the fête** — this scene is where that arc starts; lean it one way.

# CANOPIA — The Eastern Promenade & The View of the Palace

**When:** Late night, same evening (after the Dewdrop, if the party chooses the city over bed)
**Where:** The eastern edge of Canopia, looking across to the Queen's tree
**Purpose:** Easy public-layout intel for the heist · tone/scale set piece · seed the kitchen→quarters route

---

## 1 · The View from the Edge (read-aloud)

> The promenade runs you out to the eastern lip of the city, and the buildings fall away, and there it is.
>
> Across a gulf of open dark, a single tree rises so high its crown is lost above you. Light spills from a hundred windows bored and grown into the trunk — tier on tier of lit rooms stacked into the wood, balconies cantilevered out over nothing, ribbons of walkway spiraling up and out of sight. It glitters like a vertical city of its own.
>
> Somewhere up in all that warm light, the Red Ant Queen is throwing a party. And you've been thinking about how to rob it.

> **DM note:** This is the establishing "oh, *that's* the target" beat. Let them gawk. Scale it against Canopia — the whole inn district could fit in one of those tiers.

---

## 2 · The Promenade (read-aloud)

> The promenade itself is busy even this late. Bars and social clubs look down from tiered terraces overhead, their noise drifting out over the rail. Gaily lit stands line the walk, vendors calling out late-night treats to anyone strolling past.

### 🍯 What's on offer

| Treat | Vendor |
|-------|--------|
| **Whipped nectar with gold flake** | An emerald **jewel beetle**, carapace polished to match her product, working a tiny whisk |
| **Sizzling aphid on a stick** | A big, unbothered **Madagascar hissing cockroach** working a sputtering grill (*creative license: the hiss doubles as his sales pitch*) |
| **Hot spiced honeybuns** | A soft, fuzzy **tussock-moth caterpillar** with too many oven-mitted feet — *this is your intel vendor, see §4* |

---

## 3 · Passersby (color & overheard intel)

Sprinkle these as the party walks. The first cluster is pure flavor; the second cluster is **free public-layout intel** — the Queen's public-facing rooms are visible from outside and everyone knows what they are, so this is easy to come by.

### Flavor crowd
- A pair of young **luna moths** on a first date, wings half-shy, pretending to look at the view and mostly looking at each other.
- A rowdy knot of **valley carpenter bees**, arms around each other, butchering a drinking song at full volume.
- A small family of **pill bugs** — tourists, kids vibrating with that *really fun, REALLY long day at the amusement park* energy, far too wired to be put to bed.

### Layout intel — overheard chatter (free)
Play these as snatches of conversation as people point up at the lit tree:

> *"So beautiful — I heard she has **TWO ballrooms**—"*
> *"And a **fighting pit**!" "—SHH, **Jimmy**!"*
> *"…I think that one's the **great banquet hall**…"*
> *"That one with no balcony to alight on? I hear that's her **throne room**."*

> **DM note — what they can map for free:** Two ballrooms · a banquet hall · a fighting pit · a throne room (notably *the only major room with no balcony / no flight approach* — bank that detail, the players will). All visible, all public knowledge.

---

## 4 · The Vendor Intel (the honeybun caterpillar)

The tussock-moth caterpillar at the honeybun stand is chatty. **Two tiers of intel:**

**Tier 1 — free, just for stopping to talk:**
- There's a **casino** in there.
- And a **pheromone lounge.** *(He waggles his antennae. You don't ask.)*

**Tier 2 — unlocks if the PCs do something slick OR simply buy a honeybun:**
> He's got a **cousin who works the palace kitchens.** Gets him talking about the food.
- The **Red Ant Queen eats lavishly** — course after course, the best of everything.
- The **Leafcutter Queen**, though? Same plain thing every single meal: **steamed fungus from the Winter Farm.**
- 🔑 **The payload:** there's a **direct service path from the kitchens straight to the living quarters.** Built so the queens' meals never go cold. A way *up and in* that doesn't touch the public rooms.

> **DM note — the cold detail:** If your party has been to the Winter Farm, the "steamed fungus from the Winter Farm" line is a quiet gut-punch — they know what that fungus is grown on. Don't underline it. Let it sit. (See *World Codex — The Winter Farm*; the leafcutter queen is the coerced, sympathetic one.)
>
> **DM note — the heist value:** The kitchen→quarters route is the real prize of this whole scene. It's the back door. Decide what it costs to *use* (a kitchen disguise? the cousin's help? a specialist pick?) before the heist runs.

---

### Loose ends / decisions surfaced
- **The throne room has no balcony** — only ground/interior access. Intentional security feature; make sure that holds up when the party tries to exploit it.
- **The kitchen→quarters service path** — set what's required to access it.
- **Does the cousin become a contact?** (Potential inside man, or a liability if leaned on too hard.)

# CANOPIA — The Ruby Thorax (formerly *The Iron Sting*)

**When:** Daytime errand in Canopia (heist-prep window)
**Where:** A jeweler's shop on a well-kept street
**Who:** Thoralex, proprietor · Tabby's the one with business here
**Purpose:** Finish Tabby's mantis-spine blades (real mechanical choice) · soft heist intel — *the route to the Queen's private collection*

---

## 1 · The Storefront (read-aloud)

> The shop announces itself before you read the sign: flashy, but tasteful with it. Intricate silverwork frames the windows, gemstones set into the trim catching the light, the whole face of the place freshly repainted. The sign reads **THE RUBY THORAX** — though if you look close, you can see the ghost of older letters under the new paint.
>
> Not a single weapon on display.

> **DM note:** The painted-over sign is the hook — let an observant PC catch it. The old name was **The Iron Sting.**

## 2 · Inside (read-aloud)

> The interior is all soft light and velvet trays — rings sized for a dozen kinds of leg, brooches, carapace-inlay work. But behind the counter, half-forgotten on a **dusty shelf**, sits a row of blades. Expertly worked. Old craft. Clearly nobody's asked for one in a long while.
>
> Behind the counter, a tall, elegant **stick insect** — limbs like polished green twigs, rings on more of them than you can count — bends over a workbench. He's working a brilliant green stone into an elaborate **crown**, already heavy with bright-set gems. He doesn't look up right away.

**Thoralex** — proprietor. Refined, genuinely passionate about his craft, a little wistful under the cheer. Reads as apolitical; he goes where the work is, and right now the work is the Queen's.

---

## 3 · Thoralex, In Conversation

Work these out naturally:

- **The crown** is for the **Red Queen's coronation.** Most of his business these days is decorative work for her and her cohort — *"a new and interesting challenge,"* he says, and means it.
- **The name change:** Place used to be **The Iron Sting.** He repainted when the new Queen took power — *"She doesn't have much use for blades."* (Said lightly. Let the implication sit.)
- **The material:** He is **surprised and delighted** to be handed orchid-mantis stock. A rare medium. His whole demeanor lifts — this is the most fun he's had in months.

---

## 4 · The Decision — Finishing Tabby's Blades

Thoralex can do extraordinary work on the mantis material. **But the better the edge, the more it disrupts whatever strange property the stuff already has** — sharpen it, and it **loses the invisibility trick.** Leave the blade raw and just dress the handle, and the magic survives.

Lay it out as a choice:

| Option | What Thoralex does | Mechanical effect | Invisibility? |
|--------|--------------------|--------------------|---------------|
| **A — Master's edge** | Hones it to a razor's first-cut | **+4 to the first attack, +1 thereafter** ⚠️ | ❌ Lost |
| **B — Balanced edge** | A keen, durable working edge | **+2** | ❌ Lost |
| **C — Carved handle only** | Leaves the blade raw, dresses the grip | No attack bonus | ✅ **Kept** (1×/short rest, as now) |

> ⚠️ **DM — lock this number:** "+4 for the first attack, +1 after" can read two ways — **(a)** +4 on the opening strike, then a *permanent* +1 for the rest of that blade's life, or **(b)** +4 first hit, +1 only on the immediately following hit, then nothing. Tabled here as **(a)**, the cleaner standing enchantment. Pick whichever you intended before Tabby chooses.
>
> 💡 **The real tension:** This is a keep-your-toy-or-trade-up decision. The invisible blade is a *stealth/heist* tool; the sharpened edge is a *combat* tool. With the fete heist looming, Option C (stay invisible) may be the play — make sure Tabby weighs that, and consider whether she can do **one of each** if she's splitting a pair of daggers.
>
> 📝 **Continuity:** Her sheet already calls the mantis daggers *crafted* (Session 6.5). Treat this scene as the **finish/upgrade** pass, and update `party/tabatha_starr.yaml` afterward to reflect whichever option(s) she takes.

---

## 5 · The Intel — Thoralex Has *Seen* the Collection 🔑

The gold of this scene. Get him reminiscing about the Queen and it spills out:

> *"Oh, she's amassed quite a collection. I got to see it once — fitting this very piece, in fact."*

What he reveals:
- The Queen's **private collection** sits **deep in the canopy**, past the **flower-petal ballrooms** and the **hanging walkways.**
- He was led in **through the throne room.**

> **DM note — this connects two threads:**
> - The promenade scene established the **throne room has no balcony / no flight approach** — the *one* major room you can't just alight on. Now we learn *why it matters:* the throne room is the **gateway to her private vault/collection.** It's locked down because it's the way in.
> - 🎭 **The coronation is the opening.** With the ceremony happening *in or through* that throne room, the party can run **coronation-day shenanigans** to get through that entrance under cover of the event — disguise, a "delivery" (Thoralex's crown?), a planted role in the ceremony, etc. That's the heist's front door.

---

### Loose ends / decisions surfaced
- **Lock the "+4 / +1" reading** before Tabby chooses (see ⚠️).
- **Can she split a pair** — one invisible, one sharpened? (Recommend yes; it's a great compromise and she owns multiples.)
- **Update Tabby's YAML** after the choice.
- **Thoralex's crown as a heist vector** — does delivering/escorting the finished crown become the party's way through the throne room on coronation day?
- **Is Thoralex a usable contact?** He's been *inside.* Does he sketch a layout if befriended/paid/leveraged — or is that too convenient, and he only ever lets the throne-room detail slip by accident?
