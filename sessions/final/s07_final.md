# Session 7 — Heist Prep in Canopia (Final)

Reconciled from the DM-reference scene chunks ("Canopia — Return Scenes") against the
DM play notes and Tabby's (Heather's) player notes. Where the prepped scenes and actual
play diverged, this file follows what happened at the table; deviations are flagged inline.

**Framing.** Session 7 was the Canopia heist-prep day. The party spent it gathering
intelligence and setting up advantages ahead of the Red Ant Queen's fête — which they
learned is **tomorrow night**. The city is abuzz with preparations. The fête infiltration
itself is a later session.

---

## 0 · Opening — The Fête Is Tomorrow Night

The party is on the **east side of Canopia**, roughly 7pm. Word on the street: the Red Ant
Queen's fête is **tomorrow night**, and the whole city is deep in preparations.

They set out to find a **smithy** to craft weapons — and found none. Too upscale a
district for that kind of work. Nancy stopped a passing **cockroach with beautiful gem-work
on her carapace** and asked where she'd had it done. She pointed them to **The Ruby Thorax**,
over on the **west side** ("probably not open right now") — and, in passing, tipped them to
**two entrances** [DM CHECK — which two the cockroach meant: public + servants', or two the
party can exploit]. They also picked up a lead on **Trader Twizzlers**, an outfitter on the
**south side, down the street from the Ruby Thorax**, as the place to get a proper fête outfit.

> **Deviation from prep.** The prepped "Ruby Thorax / Thoralex" scene (Tabby finishing her
> mantis blades; the throne-room→private-collection intel) **did not happen** — the shop was
> closed/unvisited and Thoralex was never met. Both threads carry forward. Tabby's daggers are
> unchanged.

---

## 1 · The Dewdrop Inn — Reunion with Beeatrix

The party went to the **Dewdrop** to find Beeatrix and tell her the meadow was cleared.

She was up on the little stage, lights low, crooning a sad, beautiful lament — a song about
brave, kind bugs she'd sent off to die while she sat at the bottom of a bottle. It was about
them. **Drix joined in on his lute**; Beeatrix stopped playing, and **Nancy answered with an
interpretive dance**. The moment broke warm rather than tragic.

Beeatrix was **overjoyed** to see them alive. She **agreed to be the band's singer** (the
follow-through on Nancy's Session 6 recruitment — she has no vocalist otherwise). As thanks,
she pressed on **Drix** the last of her late mother's **Stick-All Honey**.

- **Stick-All Honey (item, to Drix).** Sticks **one thing to one thing, permanently.**
  **Single use.** (Table ruling on the prep's open "how many uses?" question.) An heirloom —
  the last of her mother's — given away freely.
- **Gold reward: waived.** No gold changs hands; the Stick-All Honey stands as the reward
  (the real payout was always the mantis salvage). Per DM decision, the gold owed is closed out.

**Band kit established:** Beeatrix plays a **lyre**; Nancy has **tap shoes and little bells**
for percussion; Drix on lute. *(Prep said "harp"; play established lyre.)*

---

## 2 · The Promenade & The View of the Palace (West Side)

The party decided to find other venues to practice and play, and went to the **promenade**,
heading **west**. The **Red Ant Queen's palace** rose into view across the canopy — a single
tree so tall its crown is lost overhead, tier on tier of lit rooms, walkways spiraling up out
of sight. It's **beautiful**. Everyone bought treats.

> **Deviation from prep.** The prep titled this "The Eastern Promenade." Play put the
> palace-view promenade on the **west** side — consistent with Session 6 canon (the palace is
> visible from western Canopia). Treat the palace as **west** of the city.

**The honeybun vendor — the session's richest intel.** A **female tussock-moth caterpillar**
working the honeybun stand got chatty. *(Prep had a male vendor and an unnamed "cousin"; play
made the vendor female and named the contact.)*

- Her **brother, Johnathy**, works the **palace kitchens** and is **working the fête** — he
  has a "special honeybun recipe."
- **The payload:** the **servants' quarters have access running under the ballrooms** — a
  servants'-tunnel understory, a way to move beneath the public floor. Johnathy is the thread
  into it.

**Busking.** The band set up by the treat stands. **Drix handed out Bardic Inspiration**, the
set went well, and Beeatrix got into it.

- **Tabby worked the crowd for intel**, pulling every overheard scrap of public palace layout:
  **two ballrooms (with balconies)**, a **fighting pit**, a **great banquet hall**, and a
  **throne room with no balcony** (no flight approach — the party banked this detail).
- **Tabby spread a rumor** that the fête is **open to everyone at 8:30pm** ("open invitations").
  It landed poorly on its own — but **Drix reinforced it** and it went over better. She
  **failed to convince the jewel-beetle nectar vendor.** *(A callback to the Session 5 casino
  rumor bit — the party likes this move.)*

**Managing the asset.** The party **talked Beeatrix into going home** rather than continuing on
to the casino — keeping her sober ahead of the fête.

> The prepped **Queen Bee Casino** scene did not run (they sent Beeatrix home instead), and
> **Vikki's Volatiles** was never visited. Both carry forward.

## 3 · Palace Security Recon (Session Close)

The party went to the **palace's grand / ground level** to scope security. What they observed:

- **Lots of goods** and materiel streaming toward the castle for the fête.
- **Guards posted every ~30 feet.**
- **Carts of strange spell components** — **juniper berries, scorpion stingers, and glowing
  goop**, among other oddments. They **don't obviously combine into anything** ("they don't do
  similar things") — it reads as **strange and magical**, like *someone is trying to figure
  something out.* **Drix cast Locate Animals or Plants** on the juniper berries.
  - *(This is the intel Vikki's Volatiles was meant to deliver — the Queen's appetite for
    "changed" reagents, pointing at a hidden lab — surfaced here by direct observation instead.)*
- A **well-fortified servants' gate with a guard tower** — "the nicest servants' entrance ever,
  for grand dwellers."
- **Enclosed walkways from the kitchen(?) to the servants' quarters** — corroborating the
  honeybun vendor's understory.

The party is **noting the whole layout with the goal of drawing up blueprints when they get
back to the Inn.**

**Session ends here** — the party mid-recon, ready to head back to the Dewdrop to compile their
intel into blueprints. **Fête is tomorrow night.**
