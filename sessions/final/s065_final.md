# Session 6.5 — The Mantis Felled (Final)

> **Scope note.** A short continuation session. Its sole content was finishing
> the orchid mantis encounter that had paused mid-combat at the end of
> Session 6 (see `sessions/final/s06_final.md` section 3). No new scenes
> beyond the fight, the loot, and the ride home.

---

## 1. The Fight Resumes (concluded)

Combat picked up where Session 6 left it — the party engaged in the flower
meadow east of Canopia, one nymph already dead, Tabby's Hunter's Mark active.
The party fought through the mama orchid mantis's camouflage-on-flower
ambushing, her adjacent-only flower hops, and the grapple-and-crush move,
finishing off the remaining nymphs and bringing down the mama.

**Outcome: the party won.** Mama orchid mantis dead; all nymphs dead; the
meadow cleared.

---

## 2. Trophies

Each party member claimed a piece of the kill:

- **Drix** — took a **mandible** (The Mantible — invisible 1d8 finesse short sword).
- **Nancy** — took a **mandible** (The Mantible — invisible 1d8 finesse short sword).
- **Tabby** — took **mantis spines** she can craft into daggers (mantis-spine daggers).

> Note: the Session 6 prep had pitched a single Mantible plus one mantis-spine
> dagger. The played result is two mandibles (Drix + Nancy) and a set of
> spines Tabby will work into daggers. The played version supersedes.

**Mechanics (DM ruling, 2026-06-14):** All three blades share the same effect —
a 1d8 finesse weapon that can **turn invisible once per short rest**.

**Barbra returned at evening** for the pickup at the meadow's edge, as
promised, and flew the party **back to Canopia** aboard her — the bee-field
side quest's combat now fully resolved.
