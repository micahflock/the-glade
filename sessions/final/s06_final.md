## 0. Session Opener — Gleam's Handoff (played)

Upstairs at the Dewdrop Inn, picking up mid-conversation with Gleam.

**Gleam's pitch (delivered in chunks across the conversation):**

> "I've been told you represent hope. That's something that the bugs of the Weevilville and the Leafcutter Queen have little of these days. I know not what information or spell you carry with you. Don't tell me! I don't want to know. But you must get it to the Queen. You come at a fortuitous moment. The Red Ant Queen has decided to hold a Fête in a few days. I am a member of the Weevilville Board of Groupees, we have been invited. I have secured invitations for your group. Your reputation precedes you — you'll join one of the bands for the evening."

He gestured broadly toward Drix, then glanced skeptically at Nancy and Tabby.

> "While there, the Leaf Queen will be brought out for a time, to be paraded around. To show her face. The Red Ant Queen has her on a tight leash. Then she will be brought back to her chambers for the evening. It is then that you will move to make your move. Sneak into the inner rooms of the Nest and make contact with her. I'm told you have some sort of token on your persons that will prove your allegiance. Again, don't show me. I need plausible deniability."

> "To help you in your endeavor I have secured aid from the Resistance in the form of some… Contacts. Bugs with special skills. They are already placed in the Nest. We can secretly send some instructions to them. But sadly not all of them — 3 of these agents can be contacted by dead drop before arousing suspicion."

**Specialist roster Gleam offered:**

- **Tudd** — Leafcutter Major. Tough, specializes in directed violence, demolition, and construction.
- **Demicous** — Leafcutter Minim. Caretaker of leafcutter babies. Outwardly meek; can get pretty much anywhere without arousing suspicion. Not who you'd want beside you in a fight.
- **Vikko** — Jewel Beetle, bartender for the event, sympathetic to the cause. Suave — good at gathering intel, poisoning drinks, sneaking supplies in.
- **Diedre** — Lacewing, one of the performers on one of the stages that evening. Great at making a distraction or providing cover for those moving in her retinue.
- **Dax** — Cockroach, first aid for the Mixed Mandible Arts cage. Could lend a hand with medical assistance or contact with a fighter.
- **Zalle** — Sawfly Larvae, pheromone-lounge mixologist. Can influence moods or apply mild status to guests.

**Dead-drop mechanic explained:** During the infiltration, the party can burn dead drops to retroactively have a contact present in a spot or to have performed an action they decide. Only 3 of the 6 can be contacted by dead drop before the event without arousing suspicion. The party did not commit picks at the table — this is owed before they arrive at the fête.

**Exit:**

> "Alright, well I will convey those requests to them. This room is reserved until the night after the event. So it's yours until then. I will send someone to fetch you at first dark on the night of the event. So be here!"

After fielding questions:

> "Alright, I leave you now. I hope we meet again, you seem fine adventuring folk. Thank you for helping those with less hope than you."

As Gleam silently closed the door, he caught his long robe in it and had to reopen it to pull the robe out. The party heard him attempt to quietly walk down the stairs.

**Party action:** They took the room. Investigated it (nothing there). Took a long rest. Rigged a sock on the doorknob as an improvised alarm.

## 1. Morning at the Dewdrop — Beeatrix Hook (played)

**Read-aloud (delivered):** As the party came down the stairs the next morning they were greeted with the sleepy scene of a dive bar during off hours. Most tables still had chairs flipped upside down on top of them. The bartender — **Dale** — was busy prepping for the day. A few patrons were seated at ready tables eating breakfast. Slumped over the bar, snoring quietly, was a decidedly rough-looking mining bee — the same one they remembered singing loudly the night before about her ancestral flower fields and fantastical monsters. Dale acknowledged the party with a friendly nod and antennae twitch.

> "Your rooms come with a morning meal. I have plenty of greenery cakes, mite meat — maybe some BBV beverages."

As Dale set Tabby's drink down, the clinking of the glass startled the bee at the bar awake:

> "MY OLE HOME IS HAUNTED BY A MONSTER, WON'T ANYONE *HIC* DO ANYTHING?"

Dale gave her a stern but knowing glare and half-mumbled to the party:

> "Wish someone would help her, just to get her to stop yelling. She used to come in and sing the classics. Now it's all she can talk about."

He walked off to pat her on the back and settle her back down.

**The party went to talk to Beeatrix.** She told them:
- The place she and the rest of her solitary bee friends lived can no longer be home.
- They are all dead, or presumed to be — there was a panic after 5 bugs disappeared in one night.
- It must be a monster. Won't someone help get rid of the monster? She will pay what little money she has left.
- Directions to the flower meadow east of Canopia. She left at the beginning of the Bloom; it must be overgrown by now. Look for lots of asters, daisies, and marigolds.

**Nancy's pivot — recruited Beeatrix to front the band at the fête.** Beeatrix used to sing the classics; the band needs a frontwoman; the party already has the band slot via Gleam. This is now an active arc element: Beeatrix is the band's vocalist at the Red Ant Queen's fête, contingent on the party clearing the mantis from her old meadow.

**Travel plan:** The party asked Dale about getting east; Dale told them they could book or find flight travel. They headed for the western part of Canopia (where the Red Ant Queen's palace is visible from across the canopy) to find an east-bound flight.

## 2. Travel to the Meadow — Barbra the Butterfly (played, replaces the prep's climb)

In western Canopia the party met **Barbra**, an Eastern Comma butterfly, nursing a hot water in a corner. She had lost all her money at the casino the night before. She told them:

- They could see the Red Ant Queen's palace from this side of Canopia.
- The flower meadow they were asking about is *very* dangerous — she knew someone who was swallowed by a flower in an instant.
- She'd take them close to the meadow for **2 sap**. She would not land in it.
- She has felt stronger since the Bloom. *(Foreshadowing — flag for the "all magic users amplified" thread.)*
- The party told her about their invitations to the fête.

Nancy wove flight harnesses to suspend Nancy and Tabby from Barbra; Drix flew alongside. The flight was *not graceful* but they were dropped on a nearby leaf — an Iris — at the meadow's edge. **Barbra agreed to come back in the evening to pick them up.**

## 3. The Mantis Fight — Begun (paused mid-combat)

Standing at the edge of the meadow the party saw **no signs of struggle**. On the underside of a leaf they spotted an **egg sack clinging to a stem**. They sneakily traveled along leaves and stems toward the eggs; on close approach, Nancy (or whoever passed the check — [DM CHECK]) recognized them as **orchid mantis eggs**.

Something shifted beside them and grabbed for **Drix**. Drix strummed his guitar and the mantis missed. **BATTLE ENSUED.**
