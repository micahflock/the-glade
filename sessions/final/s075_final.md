# Session 7.5 — The Last Day in Canopia (Final)

Reconciled from the session notes actually used at the table (the `s075_draft.md` material with
a replacement Royal Jelly Society scene) against the DM play notes and Tabby's (Heather's)
player notes. Where prep and play diverged, this file follows what happened at the table;
deviations are flagged inline.

**Framing.** Session 7.5 is the bridge between the heist-prep day (S7) and the fête
infiltration (S8). It covers the **morning and afternoon of the day of the fête** — the party
finally visiting the Canopia locations they had only heard about. Four of the six prepped nodes
ran. The session ends in daylight, with the fête still ahead of them that night.

> **Structural note.** The RJS scene run at the table was **not** `s075_draft.md §2D`. The draft
> version was built around **Honoria Pell-Mell** and **Old Bombus**, and Old Bombus existed to
> hand over a **palace construction map**. The version that ran replaced them with **Buttress**,
> **Madame Aprilis Comb-Royale**, and **Mr. Pollendew**, and its payoff is the **Aged Royal
> Jelly** instead. Honoria and Bombus are retired, unplayed. **The party has no construction map.**

## 0 · Opening — Morning at the Dewdrop

The party returned to the Dewdrop late the previous night after the palace recon. Night-time,
quiet city. They went up to their room and slept the night through without incident.

In the morning they came downstairs to find **Dale** had prepared all of their favorite foods.
He confirmed that **Beeatrix came back early the night before** — she had gone home sober as
they'd asked, and was still asleep in the Dewdrop's kitchen.

**Drix attempted to send an Invisible Servant** ahead to hold their place in the line at Trader
Twizzlers. This was walked back at the table as a rules correction — the spell doesn't work that
way — and the idea was abandoned. **Nancy took Beeatrix's measurements** instead, so she could
be outfitted in absentia, and the party set out.

> **Deviation from prep.** §1A's prepped opening — Dale looking at their clothes and asking
> whether they intend to attend a queen's coronation dressed like that — never had to fire. The
> party woke up already intending to go shopping and drove the whole day themselves.
>
> **Not reached:** the six-contacts roster handover (§1B) did not occur this session.

## 1 · Trader Twizzlers — Outfits

The line was **~40 bugs deep** and excited. The shop is **three storeys tall, narrow, and
brightly coloured**.

**The party bluffed their way in by pretending to work there** — walking past the queue as
staff. It worked. In the process they **caused another bug to soil themselves**.

> **Deviation from prep.** §2B offered four routes past the line (wait it out · "we're playing
> the coronation tonight" · a DC 13 Persuasion/Performance busk · pushing in rudely). The party
> used none of them and invented a fifth. Canon: they got in by impersonating employees.

Inside is beautiful, grand and exciting — bugs being fitted everywhere. At the centre, a
**bagworm moth seated in a cocoon chair**, working a garment: **Twizzlers** herself.

**Nancy and Drix wanted something fabulous; Tabby wanted something subtle and mobile — as
unflashy as possible.** They also asked for something for Beeatrix.

**Twizzlers asked them for a small performance.** They obliged. She liked it, and she made
their outfits.

> **Deviation from prep.** §2B.5 had the party *offering* a performance in trade for free
> outfits, with **Beeatrix singing sober** as the payoff. In play the direction reversed —
> Twizzlers requested the performance — and **Beeatrix was asleep at the Dewdrop and not
> present**. The prepped sober-rehearsal beat did not happen.

**The outfits:**

| Who | Garment |
| :---- | :---- |
| **Nancy** | Asymmetric purple |
| **Tabby** | All black and spiky — deliberately the least flashy of the three. **Has secret pockets.** |
| **Drix** | Black-to-purple ombre bellbottoms with a capelet |
| **Beeatrix** | A gown (fitted from Nancy's measurements) |

Tabby's secret pockets are **concealment tailoring** in the §2B sense — she keeps a weapon
through a casual search.

> ⚠️ **Not recorded in any input:** the **petal-seam intel** (panels strong across the face,
> weak along the seam — the technique that makes the bore-through plan work) and the **servants'
> livery** order. These were §2B's two headline payloads. `[DM CHECK — did either land at the
> table?]` If not, both remain unspent and Twizzlers is a live return contact for them.
>
> Also unrecorded: the never-wilting petal material as an in-world signal, and the rack of
> uncollected garments from Mimbleburg and Trontsburry (§2B.7, always optional).

**Settled by play:** "Twizzlers" is the **proprietor's own name**, she is a **bagworm moth**,
and she uses she/her. This closes the `[DM CHECK]` the draft raised.

## 2 · The Ruby Thorax — Thoralex

The building is a little ostentatious but still elegant. Through the window the goods look
**extremely well made**. **Nancy pounded on the door** and an **elderly green stick bug** opened
it — **Thoralex** — who invited them in after Nancy said he had an interesting weaponry
proposition.

**What Thoralex told them:**

- He has mostly been working **for the Red Queen** lately — **not weapons or armour**.
- He took them back to his workshop and showed them the **crown he is crafting** for her.
  **This is the centrepiece of the coronation.**
- He was **invited into her chambers for the fitting**.

### 🔑 The castle intel

From the fitting, Thoralex gave them a walking route through the Queen's quarters:

> **Servants' entrance → up a ramp → the great hall → up stairs to the throne room → behind the
> throne, the living quarters → up a spiral to the Queen's living quarters.**

With it:

- The **throne room has a window.**
- The living quarters behind the throne are **cozy, dim, solemn.**
- **Once past the throne it stopped feeling like a palace and started feeling like a fortress.**
- It is a **long walk from the kitchen to the Queen's apartment.**
- 🔑 **One room has an odd smell and a rumbling, sizzling sound.**

> **Deviation from prep.** §2A's headline was the Queen's **private collection / vault** — deep
> in the canopy past the flower-petal ballrooms and hanging walkways, entered through the throne
> room — and the payoff that this is *why* the throne room has no balcony. **That reveal did not
> happen.** What the party got instead is the route above. It is arguably better: it agrees with
> the palace geography in `s08_draft.md §1` (throne room as the way into the core; the spiral;
> the kitchen's distance) and it plants the laboratory as a **room they can smell and hear**
> rather than a rumour.
>
> ⚠️ `[DM CHECK]` **The throne room has a window.** Banked S7 canon is a **balcony-less** throne
> room (`s07_final.md §2`). A window is not a balcony, so these are probably compatible — but
> the throne room is the room the whole infiltration turns on. Reconcile before S8.

### The blades

Nancy asked whether Thoralex would have time to turn the **orchid mandibles into swords** and
**Tabby's mantis spines into daggers**. He would. The party split three ways:

| PC | Outcome |
| :---- | :---- |
| **Nancy** | **Kept the invisibility.** The Mantible — 1d8 finesse; invisible **1× per short rest**. |
| **Drix** | **Kept the invisibility.** Same. |
| **Tabby** | **Gave up invisibility on both.** One **Master's edge** ("strikes true on the first strike, then becomes a regular dagger"), one **balanced edge** ("extra sharp, and stays sharp after use"). |

> **DM rulings applied.** Blade invisibility stays at **1× per short rest** — Heather's notes
> record it as "invisible once a day for one hour," which is not canon and is not adopted.
>
> `[DM CHECK]` **Tabby's exact numbers.** §2A's table reads Option A as **+4 on the first attack,
> +1 thereafter** and Option B as **+2**. The draft itself flagged the +4/+1 reading as needing a
> lock and it was never locked. Party file records the effects descriptively pending your call.

**Nancy took a sketch of the crown.**

## 3 · Vikki's Volatiles

On the walk toward the Royal Jelly Society the party noticed a **recently scorched stone
building, boarded up** — incongruent with the rest of the street. **Fresh and old scorch marks.**
The sign read **Vikki's Volatiles**, and it was **open**. They went in to look.

Inside: **rows and rows of shelving, filled with bins, filled with all sorts of random stuff.**
**Vikki** — a **bombardier beetle** — was busy and grumbling, pointing at and bossing around
**Ricky, her house-centipede assistant**.

**What Vikki told them:**

- She is **supplying the wagons of goods going to the castle**.
- Her stock **has been acting weird** — big explosions, strange effects.
- 🔑 **The material going to the castle is mostly going to the Leafcutter Queen's lab.**

> **Deviation from prep — and an upgrade.** §2C's job was to name the word *"laboratory"* out
> loud, and §2D (RJS) was to carry the **Leafcutter-Queen ↔ lab equipment** link. In play **Vikki
> delivered both**, a scene early, and named the destination outright. The RJS never touched it.
> This is now the party's hardest lead on the lab.
>
> ⚠️ `[DM CHECK]` **Vikki's missing arm** appears in *neither* the DM notes nor the player notes.
> In prep the recent stump is load-bearing — it ties the scorch marks, the amplified-reagents
> thread, and her willingness to throw grenades at burglars into one image. Did it land?
>
> **Not recorded:** the "somebody else is supplying her scorpion stingers" thread, and whether
> Vikki reads as a potential ally or a defender.

**Acquired here:**

- **2 exploding peppercorns** — **20 ft blast radius**. **Nancy and Tabby carry one each.**
- **Smoke-bomb components** — powdered butterfly wings, crushed agate, and **pine resin**; makes
  smoke when burned.
- **Purple salt** — turns fire purple.

> **DM rulings applied.** Smoke-bomb recipe follows the player notes (**pine resin**, not the DM
> notes' wasp paper). Money is **not tracked** in this campaign — whether the peppercorns were
> bought or gifted is immaterial and no gp changes are recorded.

## 4 · The Royal Jelly Society, Canopia Branch

The building sits in a **garden of moss and flowers**. It was open. A **door bee outside**
welcomed them in; **two more bees inside** gave them **water to wash their hands** and **candy**.

The party said they were from **Chitinholm**. The branch replied that they had **received some
letters, but the letters "didn't have anything with them"** — meaning **flower petals**.

> 🔑 That line is the entire scene in miniature. The Canopia branch's complaint about a frozen,
> dying city writing to them for help is that the envelopes were empty of petals.

**They are collecting and framing flower samples EXTENSIVELY.**

### Madame Aprilis Comb-Royale — the branch hostess

She was **sitting by the fire**. She asked what the party had seen while travelling — and was
**concerned almost entirely with flowers**. She was **seemingly uninterested in the blight of
the Bloom elsewhere**.

> **The tonal intent landed exactly as written.** Heather's verdict, verbatim and underlined in
> the player notes: ***"She IS TERRIBLE. Literally the worst."*** Cheerful complicity achieved.
>
> `[DM CHECK]` Was she **named** at the table? The player notes only ever say "branch hostess."
> Same question for **Buttress** at the door.

### The archive — Mr. Pollendew

The hostess brought them to the archive: a **large room with a tall ceiling**, journals,
portfolios, and a **bee up a ladder** — **Mr. Pollendew**.

- They **asked him for information about The Bloom.**
- He **pointed them to a missive from Chitinholm.**
- On his desk: a **stack of opened mail and half-written responses.**

> That desk is the prepped stack of never-sent replies — *"Thank you for your concern. The flora
> here is unprecedented and the Branch is in good spirits."* It landed.
>
> `[DM CHECK]` Did the party **read** the Chitinholm missive, and is it the Dr. T'wigfingers
> cross-branch packet? Prep has it arriving sealed and dusty; the player notes describe the desk
> mail as already open.

### The jar

They found an **old cabinet** holding a **dusty jar labelled "Royal Jelly"**:

> **"Aged Royal Jelly, Bitmore batch, one dose, do not waste."**

**Mechanical effect** (per the notes used): action to consume; heals **2d10 + 10 HP** and
**increases maximum HP by 10**; for the next **four hours**, advantage on saving throws against
being **charmed** or **frightened**. Single use; the jar is afterward an empty ceramic jar.

> **DM ruling applied.** The label reads **"Bitmore batch"** — the prep's "Bittermoor Branch
> (LOST)" is superseded.
>
> ⚠️ `[DM CHECK — carried forward]` **Did the party take the jar, and who is carrying it?** The
> player notes record finding it; nothing records it leaving the building. It is **not** on any
> character sheet pending your call.

**Session ends here** — the party **exiting the Royal Jelly Society**, in daylight, on the day
of the fête. The fête is **tonight**.
