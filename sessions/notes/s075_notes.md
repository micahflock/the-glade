## Reconciliation Decisions (DM, close-out)

- **The RJS scene that ran is not `s075_draft.md §2D`.** The draft's **Honoria Pell-Mell** and
  **Old Bombus** were replaced by **Buttress / Madame Aprilis Comb-Royale / Mr. Pollendew**.
  Honoria and Bombus are **retired, unplayed** — do not add them to the registry. Old Bombus was
  the mechanism for the **palace construction map**, so **the party has no map.**
- **Smoke bomb: go with Heather's notes** — powdered butterfly wings, crushed agate, and **pine
  resin**. The DM notes' "wasp paper" is superseded. Heather's **purple salt (turns fire purple)**
  and the **20 ft peppercorn blast radius** are adopted.
- **Peppercorns: money is not tracked in this campaign.** Bought vs. gifted is immaterial; no gp
  changes recorded anywhere. Nancy and Tabby carry one each.
- **Blade invisibility stays at 1× per short rest.** Heather's *"invisible once a day for one
  hour"* is not adopted. Nancy and Drix keep the property; **Tabby loses it on both blades.**
- **Jar label is "Bitmore batch"** — Heather's reading is canon. The prep's *"Bittermoor Branch
  (LOST)"* is superseded.
- **Thoralex's intel:** the prepped **private collection / vault** reveal did not happen. The
  **Queen's-quarters route** is what landed, and it is canon. Accepted as an improvement — it
  agrees with `s08_draft.md §1` geography and turns the laboratory into a room the party can
  smell and hear.
- **Route order is not a contradiction.** §3's route was explicitly a suggestion. Played order:
  Dewdrop → Twizzlers → Ruby Thorax → Vikki's → RJS.
- **Session ended with the party exiting the RJS**, in daylight, fête still to come that night.
  The §6 closing beats (Gleam's runner, the six-contacts roster, the blueprint threshold, booking
  a flyer) **did not occur** and fall to S8 or a short bridge.
- **Twizzlers settled:** the name is the proprietor's, she is a bagworm moth, she/her. Closes the
  draft's `[DM CHECK]`.
- **Did not run:** the Queen's Crown (§2E) and the MMA gym (§2F).
