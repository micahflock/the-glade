# Session 7 Prep — Mixed Mandible Arts Roster

Companion to `sessions/s07_draft.md` §1e. Fighters in town for the Red Ant Queen's fête, where she's assembling a headline cage card. Because it's the season's event, there are **more fighters in Canopia than normal** — the party can run into them at a bar, at the **Mixed Mandible Arts gym (working name: "The Molt")**, or later in the palace cage backstage. *"Mixed Mandible Arts — like UFC."*

These are flavor-first NPC sketches. They double as **heist-prep intel sources** (anyone who has fought the Queen's cage before knows the fighting-pit zone, the under-pit support/medic room, and the backstage route) and as **optional combat encounters** if the party picks a fight. No full stat blocks here — run any fighter the party actually brawls through `/stat-block` / `/encounter-worksheet` first. Party is level 6 (3 PCs); suggested threat tier noted per fighter.

Ties to existing canon: **Dax** (cockroach) is the rebel-network medic placed in the palace cage's under-pit support room — any of these fighters who've fought there have crossed his path, which is a clean way to seed his existence before the heist.

---

## The Scene

**Mixed Mandible Arts (MMA).** Canopia's bloodsport. Two bugs, a ring of packed dirt under a petal canopy, betting on every exchange. Striking and grappling both legal; "mandibles down" is the tap-out. The Red Ant Queen runs the biggest cards, and her fête will host the season's marquee bouts — *"she just recruits some top people from around."*

**The Molt — MMA gym, western Canopia.** Sweat, resin-chalk, and the smell of old shed cuticle. Sparring rings, a weight pit, and a wall of fight posters. Run by **Old Pell** (below). Fighters drift through to train, cut weight, and trash-talk. The most natural place for the party to meet the roster all at once.

## The Roster

### 1. Krull "The Battering Ram" Borecak — *reigning champion*
- **Species:** Rhinoceros beetle.
- **Style:** Horn-first bull rushes and lift-and-slam throws. Ends fights early or not at all.
- **Look:** Enormous, lacquered black carapace polished by handlers. Wears the Queen Bee Casino's sponsor sash everywhere.
- **Personality:** Pompous, genuinely beloved, surprisingly gentle out of the ring. Believes his own legend. Calls everyone "little one."
- **Where the party meets him:** Impossible to miss — his face is on banners across Canopia for the fête's headline bout. Holds court at the casino, not the gym.
- **Heist-prep value:** Has fought the palace cage many times. If charmed/bought a drink, he'll happily ramble about *"the Queen's pit"* — the under-pit fighters' room, the medic (Dax), how the cage backstage connects to the gambling floor. Doesn't think of it as secret; it's just where he works.
- **If it comes to blows:** Headliner-tier. A genuine threat to the level-6 party head-on; built for a set-piece, not a bar scuffle.

### 2. Mireille "The Vise" Tarn — *grappling contender*
- **Species:** Vinegaroon (whip scorpion).
- **Style:** Pure grappler. Pins, joint locks, and the acrid acid-spray bluff to make opponents flinch. Has never knocked anyone out — she makes them quit.
- **Look:** Long, low, segmented; the whip-tail antenna constantly tasting the air. Calm, unblinking.
- **Personality:** Quiet, clinical, deeply professional. Respects technique, despises showboats (i.e. despises Krull).
- **Where the party meets her:** Drilling holds at The Molt. Will spar a curious PC and gently, humiliatingly tie them in knots.
- **Heist-prep value:** Knows the gym and fighter circuit cold — who's bribable, who's desperate, who owes money to the casino. Good social intel rather than layout intel.
- **If it comes to blows:** Mid-tier. Won't kill; will restrain. A grapple-focused encounter that pairs well with a non-lethal scene.

### 3. Fitz "Two-Step" Quill — *fan-favorite striker*
- **Species:** Jumping spider.
- **Style:** Explosive in-and-out striking — closes the distance in a blink, peppers, leaps clear. All footwork and crowd-play.
- **Look:** Bright, fuzzy, oversized front eyes; a showman's grin. Pops the crowd between every exchange.
- **Personality:** Warm, hungry, a little broke. Up-and-comer trying to make the fête his breakout. Easy to like; easy to recruit for a "side job" if the money's right.
- **Where the party meets him:** Loudest table at the bar, buying rounds he can't afford, or shadowboxing at The Molt.
- **Heist-prep value:** Hasn't fought the palace yet — he's *desperate* to be on the card. A natural **asset to set up ahead of time**: get him booked, and the party has a friendly face inside the cage backstage during the fête. (Possible flashback/operative seed alongside Gleam's specialists.)
- **If it comes to blows:** Low-mid tier; would rather not. Better as an ally than an obstacle.

### 4. The Cantor — *the unsettling one (Del Toro end)*
- **Species:** Assassin bug (gilt with a feeding-tube proboscis).
- **Style:** Patient, predatory clinches. Wins by "the long embrace" — holds opponents until they stop struggling. The crowd half-loves, half-fears the bouts.
- **Look:** Slender, deliberate, wrong. Moves like it's listening to music no one else hears. Never speaks during a fight; hums.
- **Personality:** Courteous, soft-spoken, genuinely calm — which is worse. No malice the party can point to, which is the horror. *Horror announces itself in absences:* opponents he's beaten don't come back to the circuit, and no one quite remembers why.
- **Where the party meets it:** Alone in a corner of the bar, or in the farthest ring at The Molt where the other fighters give it space.
- **Heist-prep value:** Has fought the Queen's cage and clearly enjoys her patronage — a thread toward the palace's darker tolerance for predators. Knows the cage layout but answers questions in unsettling non-answers.
- **If it comes to blows:** Mid-high tier and genuinely frightening; a horror-flavored encounter. Use sparingly.

### 5. Bud & Burl "The Twins" — *tag-team novelty act*
- **Species:** A pair of identical leaf-footed bugs.
- **Style:** Illegal-but-tolerated tag bouts; the crowd loves not knowing which one's in the ring. Heavy back-leg kicks (those flared "leaf" hind legs).
- **Look:** Indistinguishable. Finish each other's sentences. One has a notch in a hind leg — *maybe* the only way to tell them apart.
- **Personality:** Goofy, working-class, in it for the purse, devoted to each other.
- **Where the party meets them:** Anywhere in pairs — splitting one drink, sharing one gym locker.
- **Heist-prep value:** Low on layout, high on comedy. Good for a light beat between heavier scenes. Could be paid to cause a scheduled in-ring distraction during the fête.
- **If it comes to blows:** Low tier individually; mildly annoying as a duo. A comic-relief scuffle.

### 6. Old Pell — *retired fighter, runs The Molt*
- **Species:** Stag beetle, past his prime; one antler cracked and re-set crooked.
- **Style (retired):** Was a brawler — the "Antlered Era" champion two seasons running, long ago.
- **Look:** Grizzled, slow-moving, missing a tarsal claw. Wraps every young fighter's hands himself.
- **Personality:** Gruff, fair, sees through bullshit instantly. Has watched a hundred kids get chewed up by the Queen's circuit and is tired of it. Warms to anyone who's honest about why they're really asking.
- **Where the party meets him:** Behind the desk at The Molt, or nursing a single drink at the bar after close.
- **Heist-prep value:** **The best intel source on the list.** Fought the palace cage in his day; knows the fighting-pit zone, the under-pit support/medic room, the backstage route to the gambling floor, and which guards work the cage. If the party is straight with him about wanting to help against the queens, he'll quietly sketch what he remembers — *"the part of the pit the crowd never sees."* A clean, low-magic way to hand the party real layout intel and seed Dax.
- **If it comes to blows:** Won't. He's done fighting. If forced, he's a tough old encounter but it should feel like a failure on the party's part.

## DM Notes / Hooks

- **Pick 2–3 to actually feature.** Krull (the unmissable headliner), Old Pell (intel + heart), and one of Fitz (recruitable asset) / The Cantor (horror) will carry a gym/bar scene without crowding it.
- **Intel ladder, easiest → hardest:** Krull blabs casually → Mireille gives social/who's-bribable intel → Old Pell gives real cage-zone layout if the party earns his trust → The Cantor gives unsettling half-truths.
- **Asset to set up ahead of time:** booking Fitz onto the card, or paying The Twins for a distraction, gives the party a pre-placed advantage for the fête — in addition to Gleam's flashback specialists.
- **Dax tie-in:** any fighter who's worked the palace cage can mention the cockroach medic in the under-pit room, putting Dax on the party's radar before Gleam offers him.
