## Creative Plays

- **Nancy buying roses for the whole party** in Canopia — sweet, in-character, established a tonal note for the city.
- **Twyla scene at the fungus cart** — Nancy and Tabby helped pass out fungus rations to the line. Set up Twyla as a recurring contact and a giver of casual intel.
- **The heist pivot at the Queen's Crown.** Party announced a heist intent, sat at a high-rollers' wasp table, started with pickpocketing, pivoted to spreading the rumor about ants growing fungus on dead bodies. The wasps did not care. The flop landed exactly the way the prep predicted civilians would respond — bit of a payoff for the *"the alliance is popular"* worldbuilding thesis.
- **Nancy's exit-pickpocket** as security walked them out — 2 sap, 1 cig. Player initiative; reward small but flavorful.
- **The dead drop ran completely clean.** Bartender played it perfectly. The party didn't need any of the failure-recovery branches.
- **Gleam landed.** The table's "Gleam = THE CUTEST" reaction was unanimous. Whichever DM took him, it worked.
