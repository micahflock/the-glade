# Session 6.5 — Notes Archive

No separate DM or player notes were taken for this short continuation session.
This archive captures the post-session DM debrief only.

## Debrief Summary

Session 6.5 existed to finish the orchid mantis encounter that had paused
mid-combat at the end of Session 6. The party defeated the mama orchid mantis
and her remaining nymphs, clearing the flower meadow east of Canopia. Each PC
claimed a trophy from the kill: Drix and Nancy each took a mandible (The
Mantible — an invisible finesse blade), and Tabby took mantis spines she
intends to craft into daggers. Barbra the Eastern Comma butterfly returned at
evening as promised and flew the party back to Canopia.

The reward from Beeatrix for clearing her ancestral meadow remains
outstanding — the party has not yet collected it. With the meadow cleared and
the party back in Canopia, the campaign returns to the fete arc: the
dead-drop specialist picks, Beeatrix fronting the band, and Gleam's first-dark
pickup on the night of the Red Ant Queen's fete.
